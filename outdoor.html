Outdoor Green Walls

What it is

Outdoor green walls are living vertical ecosystems attached to building exteriors. They use soil or hydroponic systems to grow plants on walls, turning empty concrete into green, breathing surfaces that support urban biodiversity.

How it Works

Plants are installed in modular panels or frames attached to walls. These systems include:

🌱 Irrigation system (automatic water supply)
🧱 Structural support panels
🌿 Selection of climate-friendly plants
💧 Drainage system for water control

Benefits

🌡️ Reduces Heat: Cools buildings and reduces urban heat island effect.
🍃 Improves Air Quality: Filters dust, CO₂, and harmful gases.
✨ City Aesthetics: Makes cities more beautiful and natural.
🐝 Biodiversity: Attracts birds, bees, and insects.
🔊 Noise Reduction: Acts as a natural sound barrier.

Challenges

💰 High installation cost
💧 Needs regular water maintenance
🔧 Requires technical design and care

Why it matters for Smart Cities

Outdoor green walls are an important part of sustainable smart cities. They help reduce pollution, improve human health, and create eco-friendly urban environments for future generations.
