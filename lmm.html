Myanmar Materials

The Cooling Materials

01

Bamboo
And Thatch


Highly breathable, locally sourced, and low-cost. When used for walls and roofing, these natural fibers allow air to circulate freely. Because they do not trap heat like corrugated iron (zinc) sheets, they keep the living space much cooler.

02

Terracotta
And fire clayed bricks


Traditional fired clay bricks and terracotta roof tiles offer high thermal mass and insulation. Their porous nature allows them to absorb moisture and release it as a natural cooling effect, significantly lowering heat gain inside the structure.
