The Electricity Problems

01

Many
Blackouts


Myanmar currently produces far less electricity than people actually need. In early 2025 production dropped below 2000 mw in some periods. Cities like Yangon and Mandalay often gets electricity for only a few hours at a time.

02

Fuel Shortages
Old Power System


Myanmar heavily relies on gas and imported fuel. Rising fuel costs and supply problems reduce electricity generation. And much of the country's electricity infrastructure is outdated and cannot meet growing demand.
