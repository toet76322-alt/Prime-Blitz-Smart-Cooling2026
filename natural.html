Cooling Older Buildings with Passive Cross-Ventilation

Retrofitting older apartments and houses doesn't require expensive systems. By strategic placement and opening opposing windows, you can leverage natural pressure differences to create cross-ventilation.

This forced movement of air flushes out accumulated indoor radiant heat and brings in fresh, moving breezes—instantly reducing felt indoor temperatures by up to 2°C even without active fans running.
