Beating the Heat: Paint Roofing Materials for Myanmar's Power Challenges

With regular 5-hour blackout cycles across Myanmar, relying solely on mechanical air conditioning isn't sustainable. Discover how paint and roofing materials help to reduce heat during power outages.

Retrofitting Older Buildings with Reflective Roofs

For older buildings, the best option is retrofitting. Modifying the roof to use metal and white paint drastically reduces solar heat absorption by reflecting sunlight away from the structure.

This simple strategy can reduce the inside air temperature by 4°C. It delivers the same comfortable, eco-friendly environment needed in a hot climate, requiring less air con and saving valuable electricity when the power returns.
