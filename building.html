Designed for Myanmar's warm seasons and monsoon days

From Yangon’s busy downtown apartments to Mandalay’s growing residential houses, our smart cooling buildings use local climate data, natural ventilation, and smart sensors to lower indoor temperature while protecting your energy budget.

We combine bamboo screens, green roofs, solar shading and passive air flow with digital monitoring so every building learns and adapts to Myanmar’s unique weather conditions.

Powered by IoT: Smart Climate Control

In Myanmar, power grid fluctuations and high electricity costs mean energy efficiency is crucial. Our smart buildings integrate IoT (Internet of Things) sensors to minimize waste and maximize your inverter or solar battery life.

  • Automated Louvers: Open and close based on real-time outside temperature and wind direction.
  • Inverter-Synced Thermostats: Automatically reduce AC load during power outages to preserve battery life.
  • Humidity Tracking: Activates smart exhaust systems automatically during heavy monsoon rains to prevent mold and dampness.

Targeted Solutions for Yangon Living

Urban heat islands make Yangon exceptionally hot during summer. We have developed specialized approaches for both high-density apartments and standalone suburban homes.

Apartments & Condos

In densely packed townships like Hledan or Downtown Yangon, concrete absorbs massive amounts of heat. We introduce balcony micro-gardens, thermal blackout curtains, reflective window films, and smart-zoned HVAC systems that integrate with natural drafts when the monsoon winds arrive. This prevents the "oven effect" on upper floors.

Suburban & Traditional Houses

For standalone houses in townships like Insein or North Dagon, we enhance traditional Myanmar designs. Deep roof overhangs, strategically placed louver windows for cross-ventilation, double-layered roofing, and breathable clay-brick walls keep the interior naturally cool without running air conditioners 24/7.

Building Materials Optimized for the Tropics

We select and source materials that naturally repel heat, manage moisture, and support local Myanmar industries.

Engineered Bamboo

Used for exterior shading screens and interior paneling. It breathes naturally, blocks direct sunlight, and grows abundantly across Myanmar.

Breathable Clay Plaster

Regulates indoor humidity by absorbing excess moisture during the rainy season and releasing it to cool the air during summer months.

Reflective Roof Coatings

Bounces harsh UV rays back into the atmosphere, dropping the roof surface temperature by up to 15°C and instantly cooling the rooms below.

How smart cooling works in Myanmar

Because Myanmar has a tropical monsoon climate, our smart buildings are designed to move cool air naturally and use technology only when it truly helps. Wide roof eaves, open courtyards and cross-ventilation keep indoor spaces comfortable during hot Yangon afternoons and humid monsoon evenings.

  • Natural Ventilation: Carefully placed windows, high ceilings and open courtyards invite cool air while flushing out heat.
  • Solar Shading: Deep eaves, woven bamboo screens and reflective roof coatings keep direct sun from heating interior walls.
  • Digital Monitoring: Local sensors collect temperature, humidity and occupancy data so every building responds to real weather.
  • Rainwater Reuse: Monsoon water is captured for landscape cooling and evaporative shading systems.

Local knowledge and smart cooling together
