Smart Irrigation 🛰️

Smart irrigation is a modern watering system that uses technology to automatically provide the right amount of water to plants. Unlike traditional irrigation, smart irrigation systems use sensors, timers, weather monitoring, and automation to improve efficiency.

This technology is very important in sustainable smart cities because it reduces water waste and helps plants grow healthier. Smart irrigation systems are commonly used in vertical farms, rooftop gardens, parks, and urban agriculture projects.

Soil moisture sensors detect when plants need water. The system then automatically activates irrigation pumps or sprinklers. When enough water is provided, the system stops automatically.

Some advanced smart irrigation systems also connect to weather forecasts and satellite technology. If rain is expected, the system reduces watering to save resources. This makes farming more environmentally friendly and cost effective.

Applications in Smart Cities

Smart irrigation is widely used in modern cities to maintain public parks, green walls, and rooftop gardens. It helps cities stay greener while conserving water resources.

In the future, artificial intelligence and Internet of Things (IoT) technology may improve smart irrigation even further, making farming systems more intelligent and fully automated.
