About PRIME-BLITZ

PRIME-BLITZ is a student-led research and design initiative focused on sustainable smart city solutions for Myanmar. We combine green building strategies, intelligent irrigation, and responsive environmental design to make urban spaces cooler, healthier, and more efficient.

Our work brings together architecture, technology, and community-driven ideas so people can live better while conserving energy and water.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is PRIME-BLITZ?

PRIME-BLITZ is a project focused on smart cooling for buildings, sustainable water use, and urban greenery. We seek to reduce energy consumption and improve comfort through technology and natural design.

How do you support sustainable cooling?

We use vertical gardens, hydroponics, smart irrigation, and building shading systems to lower temperatures naturally while reducing reliance on mechanical cooling.

Who can join the project?

Students, educators, and community members interested in green design and smart city innovations are welcome to contribute ideas and participate.
