Beating the Heat: Evaporative Cooling for Myanmar's Power Challenges

With regular 5-hour blackout cycles across Myanmar, relying solely on mechanical air conditioning isn't sustainable. Discover how evaporative cooling helps to reduce heat during power outages.

Implementing Evaporative Cooling in Any Building

An evaporative cooling system can work well in a big dry climate, but in a wet climate, it does not work that well in Myanmar. Evaporative cooling relies on water absorbing heat from the air to evaporate, which lowers the temperature.

Because Myanmar experiences high relative humidity during the monsoon season, the air is already saturated with moisture, making it harder for water to evaporate effectively. However, during the hot, dry pre-monsoon months, these systems offer a highly efficient, low-power alternative to traditional air conditioning.

Highly efficient, low-power alternative to traditional air conditioning during hot, dry pre-monsoon months.

Limited effectiveness during wet monsoon seasons due to high relative humidity and saturated air.
